### Page bodies
The bodies are written for clinicians (statistics, acupoint codes, terms like "oligoasthenoteratozoospermia"). Suggested structure for each:

1. **What it feels like** — 2–3 plain sentences in the reader's words.
2. **How we approach it** — one paragraph; introduce *Qi* or *the underlying cause* in everyday words first.
3. **What treatment looks like** — sessions, frequency, what patients usually notice.
4. Leave the patient story and booking button to the page template.
5. Move the research (meta-analyses, mechanisms, acupoints) to the end under a heading like "For the research-minded".

Also soften "highly effective", "strongly support", "Excellent—no serious adverse events" to plain descriptions of what treatment supports.
